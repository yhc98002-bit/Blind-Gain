Arm 1 finished (last_global_step 100, five checkpoints, pid gone from an12) but
its manifest still read "status": "running". The cause is structural:
scripts/launch_m7_virl_arm.sh exec'd verl.trainer.main directly, so nothing
outlived the trainer to call finalize_manifest, whereas every other training
launcher here (launch_mech_pilot_arm.sh:311, launch_mini_a5_main.sh:230) routes
through scripts/run_manifest_job.py, which reads payload["command"] and
finalizes on exit.

Part A -- arm 1 closed with the tested finalizer, never by hand. New
scripts/close_orphaned_run_manifest.py delegates to finalize_manifest and then
records what a post-hoc close cannot observe: end_time_utc is the close-time
stamp (2026-07-30T15:45:49Z), NOT the completion time
(observed_completion_utc 2026-07-30T12:57:54Z, from the mtimes of
global_step_100, checkpoint_tracker.json, experiment_log.jsonl and an12.log);
exit_code 0 is inferred from artifacts, and exit_code_provenance says so. It
refuses any manifest that is not "running" and any run whose recorded pid is
still alive or whose liveness is indeterminate, so a job in flight is never
touched.

run_manifest_job.py, adds expected_artifacts so artifacts_exist is not vacuous,
and adds the runner and finalizer to the byte-clean CRITICAL contract list. The
command string keeps its self-contained `env ...` prefix, so the runner's
PYTHONPATH="." cannot leak into the trainer, and the trainer's argv still
carries `-m verl.trainer.main config=...`, so m7_gpu_occupancy_guard.py resolves
occupancy unchanged. FUTURE LAUNCHES ONLY: arms 2-4 were launched from the old
