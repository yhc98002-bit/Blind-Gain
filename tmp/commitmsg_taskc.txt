M5C Task C: evidence ledger for the geo3k step-100->400 turnover finding

CPU only; cached predictions plus the existing substrate. No GPU job started.
Adds reports/m5c_evidence_ledger_v1.{json,md} (16 ledger rows) and
scripts/m5c_evidence_ledger.py.

Holm-Bonferroni is applied over the 10 permutation tests computed in this
report; each ledger row carries claim / statistic / null / raw p / adjusted p /
verdict.

1. Problem-type concentration. Bucket rules were imported verbatim from
   scripts/m5c_lost_item_forensics.py and asserted equal to the rules recorded
   in the published artifact; the full bucket x {lost, gained, stable_correct,
   stable_wrong} table is emitted with exact counts and row/column sum checks.
   LOST chi2 = 11.776933 (reproduces the published statistic to 1e-9), raw
   p 0.0461 over 10,000 permutations at seed 20260729, Holm-adjusted 0.1383.
   LOST concentration DOES NOT survive correction. GAINED chi2 = 8.806222, raw
   p 0.1334, Holm 0.1383.

2. Near-miss difficulty control. The literally requested matched pool (LOST vs
   stable_correct) is DEGENERATE: stable_correct items are correct at step 400
   by definition, so their near-miss rate is 176/176 = 1.0 by construction and
   the contrast cannot test the hypothesis. A non-degenerate matched design
   (both arms correct at step 100, both scored on a WRONG answer; reference =
   dip-and-recover, wrong answer read at the first dip step) keeps the effect
   size (+0.1652 matched vs +0.1167 unmatched) but raw p is 0.0482 and Holm
   0.1383 with only 26 numeric-comparable reference items. A |gold|-quartile
   stratified version gives +0.1201, raw p 0.0148, Holm 0.0592. Also recorded:
   the published 5.0e-4 reproduces exactly under its subset-draw convention but
   is 0.0179 under arm-label permutation on the same data.

3. Margin / confidence collapse: NOT MEASURABLE. A full field census over all
   five cached per-item files finds no logprob, logit, decoding-score or margin
   field at any step; the only substring hits are three "guarded_rescore_*"
   provenance strings at step 100. Steps 150-400 carry no sampled decode at all.
   No proxy is substituted. A separately labelled side measurement (step-100
   16-sample pass rate, LOST 0.4233 vs stable_correct 0.7886, p <= 1e-4) is
   reported as its own ledger row so the margin row stays NOT MEASURABLE.

4. Five-step pattern structure. 429 observed adjacent flips vs 1197.0 expected
   under independent per-step flipping at the observed marginals (G2 = 1289.8,
   Pearson X2 = 3070.9, both above the 10,000-replicate null maxima of 70.9 and
   73.8; p <= 1e-4). Direction is MORE PERSISTENCE, not more alternation: 198
   never-correct vs null mean 26.8, 162 always-correct vs 12.7, 360 zero-flip
   items vs 39.5. Holding each item's own correct-step count fixed, 429 vs
   480.62 (within-item order permutation) and vs 480.99 (curveball preserving
   both row and column margins), both p <= 1e-4 and both below the null minimum
   across 10,000 samples, so movement is temporally ordered rather than
   scattered.
