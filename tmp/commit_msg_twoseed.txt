M7 R3 readout: two-seed capable instrument + two-seed fixture suite

Extends scripts/build_m7_r3_readout.py from a single-seed readout to a
two-seed readout (per-seed arms, cross-seed pooling, and the seed-scoped
provenance/artifact paths that go with them), and adds
tests/test_m7_r3_readout_two_seed_fixture.py, a 21-case synthetic fixture
suite covering the new two-seed paths. experiments/scratch_twoseed/ carries
the verification harness: the pre-change instrument snapshot
(build_m7_r3_readout.ORIGINAL.py), the golden probe, the seed-1 replay
driver and its comparator, the two-seed sampler, and the replay outputs
(replay/replay.json, replay/replay.md).

Adversarial verification, three independent lenses:
  1. Hand-checked arithmetic. Fixture-derived estimates were recomputed by
     hand, off the instrument's own code path, and matched.
  2. Registered-estimator fidelity. The two-seed estimators were checked
     against the registered specification rather than against the
     implementation, so a self-consistent-but-wrong estimator would fail.
  3. Byte-stability replay. The seed-1 path was replayed through the new
     instrument and compared against the pre-change snapshot's outputs.

Scope and honesty notes:
  - The seed-1 code path and its published outputs are proven byte-stable
    across this change: the replay reproduces the prior artifacts byte for
    byte, so no previously reported seed-1 number moves.
  - No real seed-2 data was read. The seed-2 arms are still training. Every
    two-seed result exercised here is synthetic fixture data used to test the
    instrument; nothing in this commit is an empirical two-seed finding.
  - Fixture suite: 21 passed
    (.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_m7_r3_readout_two_seed_fixture.py -q).
